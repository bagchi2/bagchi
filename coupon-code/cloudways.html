1. About Cloudways: The Managed Cloud Innovators

Founded in 2011, Cloudways has completely disrupted the web hosting industry by bridging the gap between highly complex cloud infrastructure and everyday users. Instead of renting you space on a traditional shared server, Cloudways acts as a powerful management layer on top of the world's best cloud providers: DigitalOcean, Amazon Web Services (AWS), and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). They configure the server, optimize the caching (Varnish, Memcached, Redis), handle security, and give you a beautifully designed control panel. This allows agencies, e-commerce store owners, and bloggers to harness raw cloud power without needing to be Linux system administrators.

2. Cloudways’ Best-Selling Products

Because Cloudways lets you choose your underlying infrastructure, their products vary based on your selection. The absolute best-seller for small to medium businesses is the DigitalOcean Premium Plan. It utilizes DO's fastest NVMe processors, making it incredibly affordable yet lightning-fast. For massive e-commerce stores (like WooCommerce) that experience heavy traffic spikes, their AWS (Amazon Web Services) and Google Cloud (GCP) integrations are heavily favored, offering unparalleled vertical scaling and raw computational power.

3. Cloudways vs Kinsta: Pricing & Features

When comparing top-tier managed WordPress hosting, Kinsta is frequently matched against Cloudways. Both offer exceptional speed and rely on cloud infrastructure. However, Kinsta forces you into specific traffic and storage limits on their Google Cloud infrastructure. Cloudways, conversely, charges based on raw server resources (RAM, CPU, Storage), allowing you to host unlimited websites on a single server, making it significantly more cost-effective for agencies and developers with multiple sites.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a Cloudways promo code?
A promotional code that provides substantial discounts, such as 30% off for 3 months or 40% off for 2 months, on Cloudways' managed cloud hosting platform.
How much can I save with a Cloudways promo code?
You can save up to 40% off your initial invoices depending on the promo code you use. Typical offers give you 20%, 30%, or 40% off for your first 2 to 3 months of hosting.
Does Cloudways offer a free domain?
No, Cloudways specializes purely in high-performance cloud hosting and does not act as a domain registrar. You must purchase your domain separately from a registrar.
Is Cloudways good for WordPress?
Cloudways is exceptionally good for WordPress. Their platform includes advanced caching mechanisms like Varnish, Memcached, Redis, and a free Object Cache Pro license, making WP sites fly.
Does Cloudways provide free SSL?
Yes, Cloudways offers completely free 1-click Let's Encrypt SSL certificates for all applications deployed on their platform, easily renewable via the dashboard.
How does Cloudways billing work?
Cloudways operates on a strictly pay-as-you-go, post-paid billing model. You are invoiced at the beginning of the month only for the exact server resources you consumed in the previous month.
Does Cloudways offer a money-back guarantee?
Because of their pay-as-you-go structure, Cloudways does not offer a standard 30-day refund. Instead, they provide a 3-day free trial requiring no credit card, so you can test risk-free.
Does Cloudways offer free website migration?
Yes, Cloudways provides one free managed migration handled by their cloud engineers. They also offer a proprietary free WordPress migration plugin for unlimited self-service migrations.
Does Cloudways use cPanel?
No, Cloudways utilizes its own custom-built, highly intuitive platform dashboard designed specifically to manage cloud servers and web apps, rather than legacy shared hosting software like cPanel.
Which cloud provider should I choose on Cloudways?
DigitalOcean is the most popular and cost-effective choice for small to medium sites. If you have a mission-critical application, AWS and Google Cloud offer unparalleled global infrastructure.
