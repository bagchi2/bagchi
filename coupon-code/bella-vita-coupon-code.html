  • Focus: Both brands emphasize safe ingredients. However, Bella Vita leans heavily into Ayurvedic formulations and a vast range of affordable luxury perfumes, whereas Plum focuses strongly on vegan, cruelty-free skincare targeting specific concerns.
  • Pricing: Bella Vita's combo offers (like 3 perfumes for Rs. 1298) often make it a more budget-friendly option for fragrance lovers compared to standalone perfume purchases from competitors.
