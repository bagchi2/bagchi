Does Spaceship offer promo codes for domain registration?
Yes. Spaceship regularly provides promo codes for discounted domain registration across popular extensions like .com, .xyz, and others. These promotions typically apply to first-year registrations.
Are Spaceship promo codes valid for domain transfers?
Yes, Spaceship offers transfer promotions alongside registration discounts. Transfer deals often offer discounted rates on popular TLDs and include a free year of WHOIS privacy.
What happens to Spaceship domain prices at renewal?
Promotional pricing from Spaceship typically applies to the first year of registration only. Renewal rates are usually higher than the discounted registration price, returning to the standard market rate.
Is Spaceship better than Namecheap?
