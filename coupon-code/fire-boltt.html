1. About Fire-Boltt: India’s #1 Wearable Technology Pioneer

By democratizing cutting-edge consumer electronics—such as Super AMOLED displays, AI voice assistants, continuous SpO2/heart-rate monitoring, and Bluetooth calling—Fire-Boltt captured the highest smartwatch market share in India, outperforming long-established global competitors.
