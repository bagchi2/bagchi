1. About FastWebHost

FastWebHost is a web hosting provider serving customers through both its India-focused website and its broader FastWebHost.com platform. The India site positions the company around affordable shared hosting for blogs, business websites, WordPress projects and eCommerce sites, while the wider service portfolio includes shared hosting, managed WordPress hosting, reseller hosting, VPS and dedicated servers. The company’s support material says FastWebHost has operated since 2001, giving it a long presence in a market where hosting brands frequently appear and disappear.

For Indian customers, the biggest attraction is straightforward pricing. FastWebHost India currently advertises shared hosting from Rs.59/month on a 36-month Basic term, with higher tiers at Rs.199 and Rs.299/month on the displayed long-term pricing. The Basic plan targets a single starter site, while Advanced and Ultimate are aimed at businesses, developers and users managing multiple websites. The India hosting page also lists free SSL, cPanel, a website builder and 400+ one-click applications, making the offering fairly accessible to users who do not want to manage a server from the command line.

That said, low price should not be confused with unlimited risk-free performance. Public feedback is mixed. The FastWebHost India Trustpilot profile has only two reviews, while other review platforms contain both positive experiences and complaints about outages, support and service consistency. That small and conflicting review sample is important context: FastWebHost can be attractive for cost-conscious users, but businesses with strict uptime or compliance requirements should test the service and read the current terms before committing.

2. FastWebHost Best-Selling Products

Linux Shared Hosting is the natural entry point for most FastWebHost India customers. The Basic plan is designed for one website and includes 1 GB disk space, five email accounts, free SSL, three MySQL databases, cPanel and a website builder. Advanced increases capacity to five websites, 50 GB storage, unlimited email accounts and 20 MySQL databases, while Ultimate is positioned for developers and allows unlimited websites and the listed unlimited storage and databases.

Managed WordPress Hosting is another important product family on the wider FastWebHost platform. Its current WordPress plans include managed WordPress administration, dedicated KVM VPS resources, automatic core and plugin updates, daily offsite backups and DDoS protection. These plans are more expensive than basic shared hosting, but the trade-off is a more managed environment for users who care about WordPress maintenance rather than simply buying the cheapest possible hosting account.

VPS Hosting is the logical upgrade for developers and growing sites that need more control. FastWebHost’s current VPS page lists KVM-based virtual machines, with entry plans offering 512 MB RAM and 1 CPU core and larger plans scaling to multiple cores, memory, storage and bandwidth. Reseller Hosting is aimed at agencies and freelancers who want WHM/cPanel and multiple client accounts, while dedicated servers are designed for workloads that have outgrown virtual or shared infrastructure.

The longer the commitment, the lower the advertised effective monthly price, but you should only choose a multi-year term if you are confident you will keep the service.

6. FastWebHost Return Policy

FastWebHost’s published terms provide an unconditional 30-day money-back guarantee for eligible shared and reseller hosting accounts when the first invoice was paid by credit card or PayPal. The policy is specifically limited to first-time accounts and monthly shared or reseller packages. Domain purchases, VPS plans, dedicated servers, administrative fees and custom software installation fees are excluded from the unconditional guarantee.

The terms also state that certain payment methods, including bank wire transfers, Western Union, money orders and checks, are non-refundable and may instead result in account credit. After the initial 30 days, eligible shared and reseller accounts may receive a prorated refund for unused time. Because refund eligibility is product- and payment-method-specific, read the current terms before purchasing a longer commitment.

8. FastWebHost After-Sales Services

After purchase, FastWebHost provides the standard management tools expected from a traditional hosting provider: cPanel on Linux shared plans, domain management, email tools, one-click applications, website-building tools and support channels. The broader FastWebHost platform also provides WordPress management, VPS upgrades, reseller infrastructure and dedicated servers, allowing a customer to scale without immediately moving to another provider.

The published terms say weekly backups are performed and only one week of backup history is kept, with additional limitations for accounts using more than 5 GB of shared disk space. For important websites, keep an independent off-site backup regardless of the hosting plan.
