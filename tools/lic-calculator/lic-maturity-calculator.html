How Does LIC Maturity Calculator Work?

The LIC Maturity Calculator helps you estimate the maturity value of your LIC policy based on key parameters. It calculates the total returns including sum assured, reversionary bonus, and terminal bonus.

Formula Used:

  • Reversionary Bonus = (Sum Assured / 1000) × Bonus Rate × Policy Term
  • Total Bonus = Reversionary Bonus + Final Bonus
  • Maturity Value = Sum Assured + Total Bonus
  • Total Premium Paid = Annual Premium × Policy Term
  • Net Gain = Maturity Value - Total Premium Paid

Note: This calculator provides estimated values based on assumed bonus rates. Actual maturity values may vary based on LIC's declared bonuses and policy-specific terms.
