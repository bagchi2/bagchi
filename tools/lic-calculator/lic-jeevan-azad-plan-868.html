LIC Jeevan Azad Plan 868 is a non-linked, non-participating, limited premium endowment life insurance policy. This means your benefits do not depend on the share market and you do not receive participation in LIC’s profits. The plan is meant to provide a guaranteed lump-sum at the end of the term and financial support for your family in case of untimely demise during the policy tenure.​

This endowment plan blends insurance and savings, making it suitable for people who want straightforward benefits and peace of mind.

Launch Date and Withdraw Date

  • Launch Date: January 19, 2023
  • Withdraw Date: January 1, 2025

Jeevan Azad 868 was available for new sales only between these dates. It is no longer available for fresh purchase, but existing policyholders will continue to receive all contractual benefits.

Key Features of LIC Jeevan Azad Plan 868

  • Guaranteed payout on maturity​
  • Limited premium payment: Pay premiums only for 8 years less than your policy term.
  • Sum Assured options: Minimum ₹2,00,000; Maximum ₹5,00,000.
  • Entry age: From 90 days (for minors) to 50 years.
  • Policy term: Ranges from 15 years to 20 years.
  • No market risk: Returns are fixed, not dependent on stock market or LIC profits.
  • Death benefit: The higher of the sum assured or 7 times the annual premium, with a minimum of 105% of total premiums paid.

Important Terms and Policy Details

  • Premium Payment Term (PPT): Equal to policy term minus 8. If you select 20 years, you pay premiums only for 12 years.
  • Sum Assured: The guaranteed lump sum you get at the end of maturity or your family gets in case of death.
  • Entry Age: 90 days to 50 years.youtube​
  • Policy Surrender: Possible after paying at least two full years’ premiums.
  • Loan Facility: Available after paying at least two years’ premiums.
  • Paid-up Value: If premiums are discontinued after two years, the policy acquires a paid-up value rather than lapsing.
  • Free-look Period: Up to 30 days to review the policy and cancel for a refund, subject to LIC deductions for expenses.​

Maturity and Death Benefits Explained

Maturity Benefit: On surviving to the end of policy term, you receive the Basic Sum Assured (₹2–5 lakh), matching what you originally chose. There are no bonuses or additions.​

Death Benefit: If the policyholder passes away during the policy term, the nominee receives the higher of:

  • Basic Sum Assured, or
  • 7 times the annualized premium

But never less than 105% of all premiums paid till death.

For minors (below age 8), if death occurs before commencement of risk, the premiums paid are just refunded without interest.​

Tax Benefits Under LIC Jeevan Azad Plan 868

  • Premiums paid are eligible for deduction under Section 80C up to ₹1.5 lakh per annum.​
  • Maturity and death benefits are tax-free under Section 10(10D) of the Income Tax Act.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is LIC Jeevan Azad Plan 868?

LIC Jeevan Azad Plan 868 is a limited premium endowment life insurance policy that provides both life coverage and a guaranteed lump sum on maturity.

What are the entry age limits for this plan?

Anyone from 90 days old up to 50 years of age can buy this policy.

What is the minimum and maximum sum assured?

The sum assured can range from ₹2 lakh to ₹5 lakh under this plan.

What are the available policy terms and premium payment years?

Policy terms can be 15-20 years. The premium payment term is always 8 years less than the chosen policy term (e.g., for a 20-year policy, you pay for 12 years).​

What are the maturity benefits of this plan?

If the life assured survives till maturity, the guaranteed sum assured (as per policy) will be paid in full to the policyholder.
