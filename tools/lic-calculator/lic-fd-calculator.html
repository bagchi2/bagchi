What is LIC FD and LIC FD Calculator?

LIC Fixed Deposit, commonly known as the LIC HFL Sanchay Deposit Scheme, is a safe term deposit where you invest a lump sum for a fixed period and earn guaranteed interest. The LIC FD Calculator is a digital tool that shows how much maturity amount you will receive based on the deposit amount, interest rate, tenure, and payout mode.​

  • LIC FDs are offered by LIC Housing Finance Ltd., a subsidiary of LIC of India, under the brand name Sanchay Public Deposit Scheme.​
  • Interest rates generally range around 6.70% to 6.90% per annum for normal investors, with an extra 0.25% per annum for senior citizens, depending on tenure and card rates.​

For official and latest details, users should always refer to:

  • LIC HFL official deposits page​
  • LIC HFL Sanchay details via partners like PolicyBazaar​

Key Features of LIC FD

LIC FD schemes like Sanchay are popular because they combine safety with stable returns. Understanding these features helps users properly use an LIC FD calculator for planning.​

  • Types of options: Cumulative (interest paid at maturity with annual compounding) and Non-Cumulative (interest paid monthly or yearly).​
  • Minimum deposit: Usually starts from ₹20,000 for the annual option and higher (for example, ₹2,00,000) for monthly and quarterly non-cumulative options.​
  • Tenure range: Commonly from 1 year to 5 years with different interest slabs like 1 year, 15 months, 18 months, 2 years, 3 years, and 5 years.​
  • Senior citizen benefit: Extra 0.25% per annum interest over the normal card rate.​
  • Loan against FD: A loan of up to around 75% of the deposit amount is usually available, subject to company rules.​
  • Premature withdrawal: Allowed after minimum lock-in period (for example, 3 months), but with reduced interest or penalty as per policy.​

These features are reflected in most LIC FD calculators available on financial portals and the official LIC HFL environment.​

How Does a LIC FD Calculator Work?

Every LIC FD calculator uses a simple interest compounding formula in the background but presents the result in an easy format. You just enter a few details, and the tool shows the maturity amount instantly.​

Inputs required

Most LIC FD calculators ask for:

  • Deposit amount (principal): Example, ₹50,000, ₹1,00,000, or more.​
  • Tenure of deposit: 1 year, 15 months, 2 years, 3 years, 5 years, etc.​
  • Rate of interest: Current LIC FD rate for the chosen tenure, such as 6.70%, 6.85%, or 6.90% per annum.​
  • Type of FD:
    • Cumulative (interest reinvested and paid at maturity)
    • Non‑cumulative (monthly, quarterly, or annual interest payout)​
  • Customer type: Normal investor or senior citizen (for extra 0.25% interest where applicable).​

Outputs shown

Once inputs are entered, the LIC FD Calculator typically shows:

  • Total maturity amount (principal + interest).​
  • Total interest earned during the entire tenure.​
  • Effective annual yield in case of cumulative options.​
  • For non‑cumulative: monthly, quarterly, or yearly interest payout amount.​

Some advanced calculators also allow you to change the interest rate manually to test different scenarios.​

Formula Used in LIC FD Calculators

Most LIC FD Calculators use standard fixed deposit formulas. For cumulative FDs (interest reinvested), the formula is usually:

  • Maturity Amount =P×(1+r/n)n×t= P \times (1 + r/n)^{n \times t}=P×(1+r/n)n×t (conceptually, though not shown to users)​
    • PPP: Principal amount
    • rrr: Annual interest rate (in decimal)
    • nnn: Number of compounding periods per year (typically 1 for annually compounded cumulative FDs)
    • ttt: Tenure in years

For non‑cumulative FDs (monthly or yearly payout), interest is calculated simply as:

  • Interest per period =P×Rate per period= P \times \text{Rate per period}=P×Rate per period​

Example shown by FD calculator explanations: If a person invests ₹1,00,000 at 7.40% per annum for 2 years in an FD, total maturity value becomes ₹1,07,400, where interest earned is ₹7,400. LIC FD calculators follow the same logic but with the actual LIC HFL rates for each tenure.​

Important Things to Check Before Using LIC FD Calculator

Before relying on any LIC FD Calculator, users should keep some practical points in mind.​

  • Check latest interest rates: FD rates can change over time, so always confirm the latest card rate on the official LIC HFL deposit page.​
  • Confirm senior citizen criteria: Extra interest for senior citizens generally applies for investors aged 60 years or above; verify this in scheme terms.​
  • Understand premature withdrawal rules: If investors may need money early, they should see how premature closure reduces interest.​
  • Tax impact: Interest earned is taxable as per income tax laws, and TDS may be deducted if interest crosses a specified limit.​

Advising users to verify every detail with LIC HFL or a licensed advisor ensures more responsible decision‑making.​

Tips for Using LIC FD Calculator for Financial Planning

A LIC FD Calculator can be used more smartly when connected to personal financial goals rather than random amounts.​

  • Define the goal first: Decide whether you are saving for emergency funds, a children’s education buffer, or monthly income support during retirement.​
  • Test multiple tenures: Try 1, 3, and 5‑year options to see which tenure gives a good balance between liquidity and returns.​
  • Use senior citizen benefits: If a parent or elder is a senior citizen, consider investing in their name to get higher interest (within legal and tax rules).​
  • Compare with other FDs: Many comparison sites show LIC HFL FD rates alongside bank FDs so users can see which option suits them better.​

Advising users to always cross‑check with the LIC HFL official brochure and consult a financial advisor is a good practice.​
