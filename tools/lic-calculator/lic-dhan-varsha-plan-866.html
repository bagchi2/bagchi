Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) often launches innovative and beneficial plans for Indian policyholders. One such popular plan is the LIC Dhan Varsha Plan 866. This plan is designed to offer a unique combination of savings and life protection with guaranteed returns, making it appealing for those looking for risk cover along with assured maturity benefits.

Introduction to LIC Dhan Varsha Plan 866

LIC Dhan Varsha Plan 866 is a non-linked, non-participating single premium policy. This means that it is not affected by market fluctuations, and the returns are guaranteed upfront without any participation in profits or losses from LIC’s investments.

  • Single Premium: You pay one lump sum amount when buying the plan.
  • Guaranteed Returns: You get a guaranteed amount on maturity or in case of death during the policy term.
  • Life Cover: The plan offers life protection, with two risk cover options.

This plan is suitable for individuals looking for a hassle-free investment with a fixed tenure, providing both savings and life cover benefits.

Launch and Withdraw Dates of LIC Dhan Varsha Plan 866

  • The plan was launched on 17th October 2022.
  • It remained available only for a limited period and was withdrawn on 31st March 2023.

Given this limited availability, LIC encouraged interested buyers to invest within the specified financial year. The withdrawal means this plan is no longer available for purchase, but existing policyholders continue to enjoy the benefits as per their terms.

Key Features of LIC Dhan Varsha Plan 866

  • Plan Type: Non-linked, Non-participating, Individual Life Insurance.
  • Premium: Single premium payment with no recurring premium.
  • Policy Term Options: 10 years or 15 years.
  • Age at Entry: Minimum age is 3 years for 15-year term; 8 years for 10-year term; maximum age can be up to 60 years depending on option.
  • Sum Assured: Based on multiples of premium (1.25 times or 10 times).
  • Guaranteed Additions: Annual additions to the sum assured.
  • Loan Facility: Available after 3 months of policy issuance.
  • Tax Benefits: Eligible for deduction under Section 80-C for the premium paid.

Two Risk Cover Options

The plan provides policyholders two options for risk cover on death during the policy term:

  1. Option 1: Risk cover of 1.25 times the single premium paid. This option offers better pre-tax returns, but the maturity proceeds are taxable.
  2. Option 2: Risk cover of 10 times the single premium paid. This option offers lower returns, but the maturity amount is tax-exempt.

Each option has different eligibility criteria related to entry age and policy term, so the choice depends on the individual’s priorities between guaranteed returns and tax treatment.​

Example Illustration Using a Calculator

Imagine Ramesh, 30 years old, wants to invest in LIC Dhan Varsha Plan 866 for 15 years with Option 1 (risk cover 1.25 times premium).

  • He decides on a sum assured of Rs. 10,00,000.
  • The calculator estimates his single premium as approximately Rs. 9,26,654 (before GST).
  • Guaranteed additions are Rs. 75 per Rs. 1000 of sum assured annually.
  • Over 15 years, he gets a total of Rs. 11,25,000 as guaranteed additions.
  • So, maturity payout = Sum assured + guaranteed additions = Rs. 10,00,000 + Rs. 11,25,000 = Rs. 21,25,000.
  • This gives an internal rate of return (IRR) of about 5.7% annually (subject to tax on maturity).

This example clearly shows how the investment grows and the benefit of guaranteed additions.​

Frequently Asked Questions

What is LIC Dhan Varsha Plan 866?

It is a non-linked, non-participating life insurance plan offering guaranteed returns with a single premium payment, along with life coverage options.

When was LIC Dhan Varsha Plan 866 launched and withdrawn?

The plan was launched on 17th October 2022 and withdrawn on 31st March 2023.

What are the policy term options for LIC Dhan Varsha Plan 866?

The plan offers two options: a policy tenure of 10 years or 15 years.

How is the risk cover provided in this plan?

Policyholders can choose between risk cover of 1.25 times or 10 times the premium paid, based on their preference for higher protection or tax benefits.

What is the minimum and maximum age for entry?

The minimum entry age is 3 years (for 15-year term) or 8 years (for 10-year term), and the maximum age at entry is 60 years, depending on the plan option.​

Are there any tax benefits under this plan?

Premium payments are eligible for tax deduction under Section 80-C, and the maturity benefits under Option 2 are tax-free, subject to applicable tax laws.
