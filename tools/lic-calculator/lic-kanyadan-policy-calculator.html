LIC Kanyadan

is not a separate product listed as “

Kanyadan

” on LIC’s official site. It is actually

LIC Jeevan Lakshya Plan 933

when bought keeping a

girl child’s future

in mind usually for her marriage or higher education.

Agents and financial advisors often use the name

“Kanyadan Yojana”

to market this plan because it emotionally connects with the idea of a father giving his daughter in marriage with full financial dignity.

Technically it is a

savings life cover plan

:

You pay a regular premium for a fixed term.

If you survive till maturity, LIC pays you (or to your daughter, as per nomination) a

lump‑sum amount

(sum assured + bonuses).

If the policyholder (father) passes away during the policy term, the

family gets a death benefit

and the plan may continue without further premium thanks to the

premium‑waiver benefit

in some forms of this plan.

Launch Date and Withdrawal Status

LIC Jeevan Lakshya Plan 933

, the base plan used as Kanyadan, was

launched on 1st February 2020

.

This plan has been

withdrawn from new sales

effective

1st October 2024

, which means LIC is

not issuing fresh Jeevan Lakshya / Kanyadan policies

after this date.

However, if you purchased the plan

before 1st October 2024

, your policy continues till maturity as per the original terms. You can still

use the LIC Kanyadan policy calculator

to estimate your maturity, death benefit, and returns even today.

Key Features of LIC Kanyadan (Jeevan Lakshya)

Here are the main highlights :

Pure life‑plus‑savings plan

: You get both

life cover

and a

lump‑sum payout

at maturity.

Flexible term

: Policy term usually ranges between

10 and 25 years

, so you can match it with your daughter’s expected marriage age.

Premium payment term

Sum assured

Death benefit

Bonus system

: LIC declares

yearly bonus

per ₹1,000 sum assured; this keeps increasing the maturity value over time.

Tax benefits

:

Premium qualifies under

Section 80C

(up to ₹1.5 lakh per year).

Maturity amount and death benefit are generally

tax‑free under Section 10(10D)

, subject to current tax rules.

Benefits You Get from LIC Kanyadan Type Plan

1. Marriage‑focused savings

The plan is designed so that when your daughter reaches a certain age (for example, 21–25), the

maturity amount

becomes available.

You can use this amount for

marriage expenses, higher education, or settling her in life

.

2. Life cover for parents

If anything happens to the earning parent during the policy term,

LIC pays a large lump sum

to the family.

This helps protect your daughter’s future even if the parent is no longer there.

3. Premium waiver in some cases

If the policyholder dies during the policy term, LIC may

stop asking for future premiums

while the policy continues to gather bonuses.

The child still gets the

full maturity benefit

(subject to policy terms).

4. Bonus and tax benefits

Every year LIC adds

bonus

to the policy, which increases the final payout.

Premiums under 80C

reduce taxable income, and

maturity proceeds

are tax‑free under 10(10D), subject to latest tax rules.

5. Loan and surrender options

After paying at least

2–3 years’ premiums

, you can usually

take a loan

against the policy or

surrender

it and take surrender value if needed in an emergency.

This gives you some

liquidity

without breaking the plan completely.

Simple FAQs on LIC Kanyadan Policy Calculator

What is LIC Kanyadan Policy?

It’s

LIC’s Jeevan Lakshya plan

(Table 933/733) customized for saving towards a daughter’s education or marriage, with life cover for parents.

Is Kanyadan a separate LIC plan?

No, it’s not separate agents market Jeevan Lakshya as “Kanyadan” for girl children to highlight marriage/education savings.

Who can buy this policy?

Only parents (father or mother) for their daughter; minimum parent age 18, daughter at least 1 year old.

What does the calculator show?

It estimates monthly/annual premium, total paid, expected maturity amount with bonuses, based on age, sum assured, and term.

What if the parent dies early?

LIC pays lump sum annual income (10% of sum assured/year) till maturity, waives future premiums.

When does maturity happen?

After policy term (13-25 years); get sum assured bonuses as one big payment for daughter’s future.

Can I take a loan on this policy?

Yes, after 2 full years’ premiums, up to 80-90% of surrender value for emergencies.

Is it still available in 2026?

Yes, Jeevan Lakshya (updated

Plan 733

since Oct 2024) is active; no withdrawal announced.
