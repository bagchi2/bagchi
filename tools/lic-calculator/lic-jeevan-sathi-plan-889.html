LIC Jeevan Sathi Plan 889 is a joint life limited premium plan for married couples, and a calculator for this plan helps estimate premium, maturity value, and death benefit more easily. LIC lists this plan as Plan No. 889 with UIN 512N394V01.

This article explains the plan in simple English, how the LIC Jeevan Sathi Plan 889 calculator works, who can use it, and what points you should check before buying. LIC’s current insurance plan list also confirms that LIC’s New Jeevan Sathi (Limited Premium) is Plan 889.

What Is LIC Jeevan Sathi Plan 889?

LIC Jeevan Sathi Plan 889 is a joint life savings plan for a married couple under one policy. It is designed for people who want both protection and long-term savings in a single policy.

The plan is available as a limited premium option, which means you pay premiums for a fixed number of years, not for the full policy term. The plan is also non-par and non-market-linked, so the benefits are fixed and not tied to stock market movement.

Launch Date and Withdrawal Date

LIC’s public insurance plan list shows Plan 889 as an active product in May 2026. A brochure dated 14 April 2026 is linked by LIC for the New Jeevan Sathi Limited Premium plan, which shows the product is recent and current.

I could not verify a separate official withdrawal date for Plan 889 from the accessible brochure text. So for now, the safest statement is that the plan appears to be active and available on LIC’s official plan list as of 24 May 2026.

How the Plan Works

The plan covers both spouses under one policy, so it is built for couples who want shared protection and savings. If one insured person dies during the policy term, the policy usually continues for the survivor with premium waiver conditions, subject to the plan rules.

The plan also offers guaranteed additions during the policy term at a stated rate, and LIC’s reporting on the product mentions a guaranteed addition of 7% of the total tabular annual premium for premiums paid throughout the policy term. That makes the plan easier to understand than market-linked policies, because the structure is fixed.

Benefits You Should Know

The main benefit is joint life coverage in one policy. That means the policy is useful for couples who want one combined plan instead of two separate plans.

Other practical benefits include:

  • Fixed and guaranteed benefits, not market-linked returns.
