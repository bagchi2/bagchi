Health is wealth, and protecting yourself and your family from health emergencies is essential. The Life Insurance Corporation of India’s (LIC) Arogya Rakshak Plan 906 is a special health insurance product designed to provide financial support during hospitalization and surgeries.

Introduction to LIC Arogya Rakshak Plan 906

LIC launched the Arogya Rakshak Plan 906 on 19th July 2021. It is a non-linked, non-participating, individual health insurance plan that provides fixed benefits during hospitalization and surgeries irrespective of the actual medical expenses. This means that you receive a lump sum amount based on the sum insured, not just the bills you submit. It supports you financially in medical emergencies without requiring expense proof for reimbursement.

The plan covers the principal insured (policyholder), spouse, children, and parents in one policy. It is generally available until the insured attains the age of 80 years (depending on the category of insured).

As of now, there has been no official withdrawal date announced for this plan, and it continues to be available for purchase.

Unique Features of Arogya Rakshak Plan 906

This plan is different from most health insurance policies because it works on a fixed benefit basis rather than hospital expense reimbursement. Here are some of the key features:

  • Fixed Benefits: You get lump-sum cash benefits on hospitalisation and surgery irrespective of actual costs.
  • Coverage for Multiple Family Members: The principal insured can cover a spouse, children, and parents under a single policy.
  • Auto Step-Up Benefit: Benefits increase automatically by 10% every year for the first 5 years.
  • No Claim Bonus: A bonus is awarded for every claim-free year.
  • Health Check-up Benefit: Periodic health check-ups are covered.
  • Extended Hospitalisation Benefit: Additional benefits if hospitalisation extends beyond 10 days.
  • Quick Cash Facility: Immediate cash support on hospitalisation.
  • Optional Riders: You can add riders like Term Assurance and Accident Benefit for enhanced protection.
  • Policy Term and Modes: Premiums can be paid yearly or half-yearly, and coverage continues till age 80 for adults.

Benefits in Detail

Let’s break down the major benefits this plan offers:

Hospital Cash Benefit (HCB)

When hospitalised for more than 24 hours, you get a fixed daily cash benefit starting from Rs 2,500 up to Rs 10,000 per day depending on your chosen daily cash benefit amount. This amount is provided for a maximum number of days indicated in the policy.

Major Surgical Benefit (MSB)

For surgeries that meet specified criteria, the plan pays a lump sum amount based on the surgery category. This benefit is paid once per surgery and can be claimed multiple times for different surgeries in the policy term.

Day Care Procedure Benefit (DCP)

This benefit covers surgeries or treatments that do not require 24 hours of hospitalisation but are medically necessary. They are covered with fixed lump-sum benefits.

Other Surgical Benefit (OSB)

Covers minor surgeries and procedures with defined fixed benefits as per policy guidelines.

Medical Management Benefit (MMB)

Supports expenses related to hospitalisation and medical management as a fixed cash amount.

Extended Hospitalization Benefit (EHB)

If hospitalisation extends beyond 10 days, an additional fixed amount is paid to help manage extended medical costs.

Additional Benefits

  • Auto Step-Up Benefit: Yearly increase of 10% in fixed benefits for the first 5 years.
  • No Claim Bonus: Increase in benefits for every claim-free year.
  • Health Check-up Benefit: Periodic health checkups included.
  • Auto Health Cover Benefit: In case of the death of the original principal insured, coverage is extended automatically to surviving family members.

Additional Riders and Benefits

Apart from core benefits, optional riders can enhance coverage:

  • Term Assurance Rider: Provides life cover on the principal insured along with health benefits.
  • Accident Benefit Rider: Offers extra benefits in case of accidental injuries or death.
  • Premium Waiver Benefit: Waives premiums in case of specified events.

These add-ons allow customisation based on your needs and budget.

Advantages of LIC Arogya Rakshak Plan 906

  • Family cover under one plan for convenience.
  • Auto-increase and bonuses enhance value over time.

Conclusion

In conclusion, LIC Arogya Rakshak Plan 906 is a comprehensive health insurance policy designed to provide fixed, lump-sum benefits during hospitalisation, major surgeries, day care procedures, and extended hospitalisation, irrespective of actual medical expenses. Frequently Asked Questions

What is LIC Arogya Rakshak Plan 906?
It is a fixed-benefit health insurance plan by LIC that provides lump-sum benefits during hospitalisation and surgeries, regardless of actual medical expenses.
When was the Arogya Rakshak Plan 906 launched?
The plan was launched on 19th July 2021 and is currently active for new enrolments.
Who can buy this plan?
Any healthy individual aged between 18 and 65 years can opt in as the Principal Insured. It also allows coverage of a spouse, children (from 91 days to 20 years), and parents under one policy.
What benefits does this plan offer?
It offers a hospital cash benefit, a major surgical benefit, a day care procedure benefit, a medical management benefit, and an extended hospitalisation benefit along with auto step-up and no-claim bonuses.
Is this a reimbursement policy?
No, it pays a fixed lump-sum cash benefit during hospitalisation/surgery, independent of actual hospital bills.
Can I buy this plan for my whole family?
Yes, the plan covers multiple lives under a single policy, including spouse, children, and parents.
