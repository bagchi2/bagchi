Insurance is an essential part of financial planning, offering protection and assured savings. When it comes to reliable life insurance in India, LIC (Life Insurance Corporation of India) is a trusted name. One of its newest offerings is the LIC Nav Jeevan Shree Plan 912, designed for those seeking a safe and flexible way to combine life cover with guaranteed savings.

What is LIC Nav Jeevan Shree Plan 912?

LIC Nav Jeevan Shree Plan 912 is a limited premium, non-linked, non-participating endowment plan introduced by LIC. It is a plan where the policyholder pays premiums for a limited duration but receives life cover and benefits for a longer policy term.

The key features include:

  • Premium Payment Term (PPT): Choose to pay premiums for 6, 8, 10, or 12 years.
  • Policy Term: Ranges from 10 to 20 years, based on the premium payment term.
  • Guaranteed Additions: A fixed percentage of the annual premium is added to the policy every year.
  • Life Cover: Death benefit is payable as per the chosen option by the policyholder.
  • Flexible Payout Options: Maturity and death benefits can be paid as lump sums or installments.
  • Loan Facility: The policyholder can avail himself of loans against this policy during the term.
  • Minimum Entry Age: 30 days; Maximum Entry Age: 60 years (depending on the PPT and policy term).
  • Minimum Sum Assured: ₹5,00,000 with no upper limit.

The plan is designed to help policyholders accumulate savings with guaranteed additions while providing life insurance protection for their family.

Why Choose LIC Nav Jeevan Shree Plan 912?

This plan is ideal for individuals looking for:

  • Guaranteed Savings: The plan offers a guaranteed addition to the sum assured every year, giving a predictable growth in the policy value.
  • Limited Premium Payment: Unlike traditional plans with premiums payable throughout the policy term, here you can pay premiums for a short period and remain covered for a longer duration.
  • Flexibility in Terms: You can customize your policy term and premium payment duration depending on your financial capacity.
  • Life Cover with Security: Your family gets assured financial support in case of death.
  • Option for Payouts in Installments: Beneficiaries have the flexibility to receive death benefits in installments, easing financial management.
  • Loan Facility: Helps in emergencies without breaking your savings plan.

Features and Benefits of LIC Nav Jeevan Shree Plan 912

Guaranteed Additions

One of the standout benefits of this plan is the guaranteed additions credited annually on the premiums paid. It does not depend on market fluctuations, offering reliable savings growth. The guaranteed addition percentage depends on the policy term as below:

These additions are accumulated and paid either on maturity or in the event of death during the policy term.

Death Benefit Options

The plan offers two options regarding the death benefit payout to suit the policyholder’s financial goals:

  • Option 1: The death benefit payable is the higher of 7 times the annual premium or the basic sum assured.
  • Option 2: The death benefit payable is the higher of 10 times the annual premium or the basic sum assured.

Additionally, death benefits can be received as:

  • A lump sum amount
  • Installments payable over 5, 10, or 15 years (this installment payout can be chosen at inception or during the policy term).

Maturity Benefit

On survival till the end of the policy term, the policyholder receives:

  • The basic sum assured
  • Guaranteed additions accrued over the years

The total maturity benefit is the sum of the above amounts.

Premium Payment Flexibility

Policyholders can choose to pay premiums annually for 6, 8, 10, or 12 years based on their budget and needs. The policy term extends beyond the premium payment term, up to 20 years.

Loan Facility

The policyholder can avail loans against the policy after a stipulated period, providing financial liquidity in emergencies.

Tax Benefits

Premiums paid and benefits received are eligible for tax benefits under prevailing Indian tax laws, subject to conditions.

Important Points to Remember

  • Suicide Clause: If the insured commits suicide within 12 months from policy start or revival, only a refund of 80% of premiums paid is payable.
  • Policy Revival: Lapsed policies can be revived under certain conditions.
  • No Market Risk: The plan is non-linked; guaranteed additions are irrespective of market performance.
  • Tax Rules: Tax benefits and liabilities should be checked with current tax laws.
