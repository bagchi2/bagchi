LIC New Bima Bachat Plan 916 Calculator

LIC New Bima Bachat Plan 916 is described in the uploaded source as a single premium, participating, non-linked, money back plan. The source states a launch date of 1st February 2020 and a withdrawal date of 1st January 2025.

Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) has been a trusted name in the insurance sector for decades, offering a variety of plans to secure the financial future of individuals and their families. One such plan that stood out in recent years was the LIC New Bima Bachat Plan 916.

This plan offered a unique blend of savings and protection with attractive survival benefits. Though the plan has been withdrawn recently, understanding its features and how to calculate premiums and maturity value through a calculator remains valuable.

Introduction to LIC New Bima Bachat Plan 916

Launched on 1st February 2020, the LIC New Bima Bachat Plan 916 was designed as a single-premium money-back plan. It provided policyholders with financial protection against unfortunate death during the policy term and periodic survival benefits. If the policyholder survived till maturity, they received the single premium paid along with loyalty additions.

However, this plan was withdrawn on 1st January 2025, meaning no new policies are being sold under this plan anymore, but existing policies continue as per the agreed terms.

Key Features of LIC New Bima Bachat Plan 916

  • Single Premium Payment: The entire premium is paid upfront.
  • Money-back Benefits: 15% of the sum assured is paid every 3 years as a a survival benefit.
  • Policy Terms: Available for terms of 9, 12, and 15 years.
  • Death Benefit: In case of death during the policy term, the nominee receives the full sum assured.
  • Loyalty Addition: On maturity, along with the single premium, loyalty additions (if any) are paid.
  • Loan Facility: Policyholders can avail of loans up to 90% of the surrender value to meet liquidity needs.
  • Free Look Period: 15 days to review the policy and cancel if not satisfied.
  • Minimum and Maximum Ages: Entry age from 15 years to 50 years (nearer birthday).
  • Minimum Sum Assured: Rs. 35,000 for 9 years; Rs. 50,000 for 12 years; Rs. 70,000 for 15 years.
  • No Maximum Sum Assured Limit.

Eligibility Criteria

Benefits of LIC New Bima Bachat Plan 916

  1. Financial Protection to Nominee: In case of untimely death during the policy tenure, the nominee gets the full sum assured.
  2. Regular Survival Benefits: 15% of the sum assured is paid every 3 years, ensuring periodic returns.
  3. Return of Single Premium at Maturity: On survival till maturity, the policyholder gets back the full single premium paid.
  4. Loyalty Additions: Additional bonus paid at maturity as a reward for staying invested.
  5. Loan Facility: Access to a loan of up to 90% of the surrender value for emergencies.
  6. Tax Benefits: Premiums paid and maturity proceeds are eligible for tax benefits under the Income Tax Act (subject to prevailing tax laws).

How Does LIC New Bima Bachat Plan 916 Work?

The plan works on a single premium basis. When you pay the premium, the policy starts, and the benefits unfold as follows:

  • At the end of every 3 years during the policy term, 15% of the sum assured is paid as a survival benefit.
  • If the policyholder passes away anytime during the tenure, the full sum assured is paid to the nominee, irrespective of how many survival benefits were already paid.
  • If the policyholder survives the entire policy term, the maturity benefit consists of the single premium paid plus any loyalty additions.

Withdrawn Status and What It Means

It is essential to note that LIC New Bima Bachat Plan 916 was withdrawn on 1st January 2025. The withdrawal means:

  • New policies cannot be purchased under this plan.
  • Existing policyholders continue as per plan terms.
  • Premium payment and benefits remain unchanged for ongoing policies.
