LIC Jeevan Anand plans offer lifelong protection and savings for Indian families. These policies from Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) combine insurance cover with maturity benefits, and calculators help estimate premiums and returns.

What is LIC Jeevan Anand?

LIC Jeevan Anand is a popular endowment plan that gives life cover during the policy term and continues protection even after maturity. You pay premiums for a set period, get a lump sum at maturity with bonuses, and your family receives the sum assured if you pass away.

This plan suits those wanting security and savings. It is a participating policy, so you earn bonuses declared by LIC each year.

History and Policy Variants

LIC launched the original Jeevan Anand (Plan 149) on January 17, 2002. It was withdrawn around 2013.

Key variants include:

  • Plan 149: Old version, endowment plus whole life, minimum age 18, maximum maturity age 100 years.
  • Plan 815 (New Jeevan Anand): Launched post-2013, term 15-35 years, age entry 18-50 years.
  • Plan 915: Launched February 1, 2020, withdrawn October 1, 2024.
  • Plan 715: Current version from October 1, 2024, UIN 512N279V03, ongoing sales.

Plan 715 replaced 915 with minor updates. All provide similar benefits: maturity payout and lifelong cover.

Key Features Across All Plans

All Jeevan Anand policies share core features.

  • Lifelong Cover: After maturity, the basic sum assured pays on death.
  • Bonuses: Simple reversionary bonuses yearly, plus a final additional bonus (FAB) at maturity or death.
  • Premium Modes: Yearly, half-yearly, quarterly, and monthly.
  • Riders: Optional accidental death/disability, critical illness.
  • Loan Facility: After 3 full premiums.
  • Tax Benefits: Deductions under Section 80C and maturity tax-free under 10(10D).

The minimum sum assured starts at Rs 1 lakh.

Detailed Explanation of Each Policy

Plan 149 (Original Jeevan Anand)

Launched in 2002, withdrawn in 2013. Premium-paying term, 5-57 years; term till age 100. Death benefit before maturity: sum assured + bonuses. Post-maturity: sum assured only. Surrender after 3 years.

Example: Age 30, sum assured Rs. 10 lakh, 25-year PPT, yearly premium ~Rs. 41,206, maturity ~Rs. 26.35 lakh (with bonuses).

Plan 815 (New Jeevan Anand)

2013 version. Entry age: 18-50, term: 15-35 years, max maturity: 70. Maturity: sum assured, vested bonuses, FAB. Death benefit: 125% of the sum assured if within the first 5 years, else the higher of the sum assured or 105% of total premiums + bonuses.

The accidental rider adds extra coverage up to Rs. 5 lakh.

Plan 915

Launched February 1, 2020, withdrawn October 1, 2024. Similar to 815 but with updated rates. Featured dual benefits and profit sharing.

Plan 715 (Latest New Jeevan Anand)

Available since October 1, 2024. Same structure as 915, UIN 512N279V03. Sample: Age 26, Rs 10 lakh sum, 21-year term, first-year premium Rs 56,242, maturity ~Rs 20.66 lakh including bonuses and FAB.​

Death sums are assured, often 125% of the basic.

Who Should Buy Jeevan Anand?

Ideal for conservative savers, parents planning a child’s future, or those needing lifelong cover. Avoid if seeking high returns or pure protection.

Minimum entry age is 18, max 50 for new plans.

Surrender and Paid-Up Options

Surrender after 3 premiums: Guaranteed value (30% total premiums minus the first year) or special value, whichever is higher.

Paid-up if premiums lapse: Reduced benefits proportional to paid premiums.​

Revival within 5 years.

Tax and Legal Notes

Premiums qualify for an 80C deduction up to Rs. 1.5 lakh. Maturity is tax-free if the premium is <10% of the sum assured.

Consult the advisor for the latest rules.

FAQs

What is the minimum premium for Jeevan Anand?

Around Rs. 20,000-30,000 yearly for a Rs. 5 lakh sum, depending on age/term.

Does cover continue after age 100?

Plan 149, yes (to death); newer plans till maturity, then sum assured lifelong.

Are bonuses guaranteed?

No, declared annually by LIC.

Can I add riders?

Yes, accidental death and critical illness.

How to calculate surrender value?

Use online tools; higher after more premiums/bonuses.

Is Plan 715 better than 915?

Similar, minor rate tweaks; current offering.​

What is FAB?

One-time bonus at maturity/death, e.g., Rs. 10,000-1 lakh based on term.
