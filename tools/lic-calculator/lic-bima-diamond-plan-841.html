Life Insurance Corporation (LIC) has long been a trusted name in India for life insurance and investment plans. One such plan that many people have found useful is the LIC Bima Diamond Plan 841.

What is LIC Bima Diamond Plan 841?

LIC Bima Diamond Plan 841 is a non-linked, with-profit, money-back life insurance plan. It combines life cover with regular returns and a lump sum maturity benefit. The policy term and premium payment term are different, meaning you pay premiums for fewer years than the total duration of the policy.

Key highlights include:

  • Limited premium payment term: Pay premiums for 10, 12, or 15 years depending on your policy term.
  • Policy term options: 16, 20, or 24 years.
  • Money-back benefits: A percentage of the sum assured is returned at regular intervals during the policy term.
  • Extended risk cover: Even after your policy matures, life cover continues for half the policy term.
  • With-profit plan: The policy participates in profits (bonuses declared by LIC).
  • Available for a limited time and usually no longer sold after 2017 (but valuable for existing policyholders).

It’s designed as a combination of protection and savings, making it suitable for those looking for periodic payouts without sacrificing life cover.

Key Features of LIC Bima Diamond Plan 841

Here are some important features of the plan:

Benefits of LIC Bima Diamond Plan 841

Survival Benefits (Money Back)

On surviving specified policy years (after every 4th year), policyholders receive 12% of the sum assured as periodic payouts called money-backs. The number of payouts depends on the policy term chosen.

  • For 16-year policy term: Survival benefit paid 3 times.
  • For 20-year policy term: Survival benefit paid 4 times.
  • For 24-year policy term: Survival benefit paid 5 times.

Maturity Benefit

At the end of the policy term, the policyholder receives the remaining sum-assured amount (40% or 55% based on the term) along with any loyalty additions or bonuses declared by LIC.

Death Benefit

If the policyholder dies during the policy term, the nominee receives the sum assured on death plus any accrued bonuses. The plan also offers an extended risk cover after maturity, ensuring continued protection.

Extended Cover Period

A unique feature is the extended cover period, which provides life insurance cover for a period equal to half of the original policy term beyond maturity, without requiring premium payments.

Suppose Mr. Aravind, aged 25, opts for a 24-year policy term with a sum assured of Rs. 5,00,000 and a premium-paying term of 15 years. Using a typical calculator:

This example shows how the plan balances premiums with multiple payouts and a lump sum maturity amount.

Why Choose LIC Bima Diamond Plan 841?

  • Regular income during policy term: Money back benefits every 4 years help with liquidity.
  • Long-term protection with extended cover: Life cover beyond maturity provides peace of mind.
  • Tax advantages: Premiums are tax-deductible; maturity and death claims are tax-free.
  • Flexible premium payment modes: Pay yearly, half-yearly, quarterly, or monthly.
  • Loan facility: Get a loan against your policy after 3 years for emergencies.
  • Trusted LIC reliability: LIC’s strong claim settlement history assures reliability.

Important Things to Remember

  • This is a closed plan, mostly not available for fresh sale after 2017.
  • It is a non-linked plan, meaning returns are not affected by market fluctuations.

FAQs on LIC Bima Diamond Plan 841

What is LIC Bima Diamond Plan 841?

LIC Bima Diamond Plan 841 is a money-back life insurance plan that offers periodic payouts during the policy term along with life coverage and maturity benefits.

Who is eligible to buy this plan?

Applicants must be between the age of 14 and 50 years (depending on chosen policy term) to buy LIC Bima Diamond Plan 841.

What is the policy term and premium payment term?

The policy term can be 16, 20, or 24 years while the premium payment term is shorter at 10, 12, or 15 years respectively.

How are the money back benefits paid?

Money back benefits equal to 12% of the sum assured are paid every 4 years during the policy term.

What happens if the policyholder dies during the term?

In case of death, the nominee receives the sum assured on death plus any accrued bonuses or loyalty additions.
