What is a LIC term plan?

An LIC term plan is a simple life insurance policy. You pay premiums for a set number of years, and if something happens to you during that time, your family gets a big sum of money called the sum assured. No money back if you outlive the term that keeps premiums low.

These plans focus on pure protection. They suit young earners in India who want to cover loans, kids’ education, or daily needs. LIC, as the biggest insurer, offers trusted options.

Use a calculator to enter your age, cover amount, and term. It shows yearly or monthly premiums right away.

Why Use a Term Plan Calculator?

Current LIC Term Plans List

LIC has eight active term plans as of January 2026. Here they are with key details. Some older ones, like Tech Term 854, got replaced.

No withdrawal dates for these yet; check the LIC site for updates.

Plan 876: LIC’s Digi Term

Digi Term is an online-only plan for quick buy. Entry age: 18-45 years (last birthday); term up to age 75. The sum assured starts at Rs 50 lakh, with no upper limit.

Death benefit: Full sum assured or 7x annual premium or 105% premiums paid, whichever is higher. No maturity payout. Premiums are lower for level or increasing cover.

Launched around 2023, still active. Buy direct on licindia.in. Good for tech-savvy users.

Plan 878: LIC’s Digi Credit Life

LIC’s Digi Credit Life covers loans like home or personal ones. Non-linked, pure risk plan. The decreasing sum assured matches the loan balance, starts high, and drops yearly.

Entry 18-45, term 5-30 years. Online only, special woman’s rates. Death pays the outstanding principal at year-start. No survival benefit.

Launched 2024. Use the calculator for loan amount and interest rate to see the schedule.

Plan 877: LIC’s Yuva Credit Life

Like LIC’s Yuva Credit Life but for youth. Pure decreasing term for loans. Choose a basic sum assured, a term of 5-30 years, and an interest rate for the risk schedule.

Launched Aug 6, 2024. Death benefit: The higher of 105% of premiums or the basic sum assured (adjusted down). Offline buying is possible. Min age 23 maturity? No, entry 18-45.

Plan 875: LIC’s Yuva Term

LIC’s Yuva Term Offline youth term plan. Level or increasing sum assured. The first 5 years are fully covered, and then it grows 10% yearly up to the 15th year or so.

Entry 18-45, max maturity 75. Death: Higher of 7x premium, 105% paid, or absolute assurance. Launched 2024. Affordable for starters.

Plan 954: LIC’s New Tech-Term

Updated online LIC’s New Tech-Term replacing 854 (withdrawn). Non-par, pure risk. Level or increasing options. Entry 18-65, term 10-40, maturity to 80.

Single/limited/regular pay. Surrender value for single/limited after lock-in. Free look for 30 days.

Launched post-2023 withdrawal of the old tech term.

Plan 955: LIC’s New Jeevan Amar

Offline pure LIC’s New Jeevan Amar . Min Rs 25 lakh assured. Entry 18-65, term 10-40. Regular or limited pay (minus 5/10 years).

Death is higher for formulas like 7x annual or assured. Riders available. Example: A 35-year-old with a Rs 50 lakh 20-year term nominee gets full if early death. Launched 2024-25.

Plan 859: LIC’s Saral Jeevan Bima

Simplest, cheapest LIC’s Saral Jeevan Bima term. Sum assured: Rs 5-25 lakh only. Entry 18-65, term 5-40 to age 70. Standard rates, no extras needed.

Launched Apr 16, 2021, still going strong. For low-income families. Waiting period for suicide.

Plan 887: LIC’s Bima Kavach

LIC’s Bima Kavach lifetime cover to 100 years? Flexible increases at life events like marriage (50% up to Rs 2 Cr). Level sum assured base. Riders for accidents.

Regular/limited/single pay. Entry varies: under 40 for boosts. Launched recently in 2024-25. Great for long-term family protection.

Common Policies Across LIC Term Plans

All are non-par, with no bonuses. Death benefit standard: Higher of sum assured, 7x annual premium (10x single), or 105% total premiums.

Premium modes: yearly, half-yearly, quarterly, and monthly (extra 2% for monthly). Grace: 30 days. Lapse revival in 5 years. Suicide: 80% premiums first year.

Free look: 15-30 days to cancel with refund minus charges. Nomination must be. Tax: Premiums 80C, death tax-free 10 (10D). Riders: Accident benefit up to Rs 1 Cr total.

No loans or surrender usually, except limited/single pay. GST 4.5% on premiums over Rs 2 lakh single.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I buy multiple LIC term plans?

Yes, but total cover limits apply for riders. Stack for more protection.

What if I miss premiums?

Grace period of 30 days, then paid up; if 3+ years paid, reduced cover. Revive within 5 years.

Are premiums fixed?

Yes, guaranteed for the term. No hikes unless the smoker is undeclared.

Women discount?

Yes, 5-10% lower in most plans.

Tax benefits?

Section 80C deduction up to Rs 1.5L; death proceeds are tax-free.

What does the calculator estimate?

It provides an estimated premium using the plan rates and adjustment factors contained in the source calculator. Actual LIC quotations can differ.

Does smoking status affect the estimate?

Yes. The source calculator uses separate smoker and non-smoker rate assumptions.

Can I select increasing cover?

Yes. Select Increasing Cover in the calculator to apply the source calculator's increasing-cover adjustment.

Is GST included?

Yes. The source calculation adds 18% GST to the computed base premium.

Can I use limited-pay options?

Yes. The calculator includes 5-year, 10-year and 15-year limited payment options, along with regular and single premium choices.

Does age change the premium?

Yes. The source model applies age-based factors to the selected base rate.

Does the calculator support female rates?

Yes. The source data contains separate male and female rate assumptions for every listed plan.

Which plans are included?

How often should I compare term plans?

Compare when your income, financial liabilities, family responsibilities or desired cover changes.

Is this an official LIC quotation?

No. This is an estimate based on the source calculator's assumptions and should not be treated as an official LIC quotation.
