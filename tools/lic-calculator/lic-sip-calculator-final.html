What is a SIP? Understanding Systematic Investment Plan

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to invest a fixed amount of money every month in mutual funds. By investing small amounts regularly, investors can build a significant corpus over time, benefiting from the power of compounding and rupee cost averaging. SIPs are an excellent tool to develop a habit of disciplined savings while avoiding the risk of market timing.

LIC, being one of India’s largest and most trusted insurance and investment companies, offers mutual funds in which you can invest via SIPs. The LIC SIP Calculator specifically helps estimate how your monthly investments in LIC mutual funds could grow over a chosen period.

What is an LIC SIP Calculator?

The LIC SIP Calculator is an online financial tool that allows you to estimate expected returns on your monthly investments made through SIP in LIC mutual funds.

This calculator provides a projection of the maturity amount you might get at the end of your investment tenure by considering your monthly SIP amount, the period of investment, and the expected rate of return.

How Does the LIC SIP Calculator Work?

The LIC SIP Calculator is based on a financial formula that calculates the future value of a series of monthly investments considering compound interest. The formula generally used is: A=P×(i(1+i)n−1)×(1+i)

Where:

  • A = Maturity amount (the future value of your investment)
  • P = Monthly SIP amount
  • i = Monthly rate of interest (annual expected return divided by 12 and expressed in decimal)
  • n = Total number of SIP instalments (months)

Simplified Explanation:

  • You invest a fixed amount in P every month.
  • Each instalment earns interest at an expected rate i, compounded every month.
  • After n months, the total value of A depends on your monthly contributions and compounded returns.

Step-by-Step Guide to Using the LIC SIP Calculator

Using the LIC SIP Calculator is simple. Here’s how to do it:

  1. Enter Your Monthly SIP Amount: Decide how much you can invest every month. Even small amounts like ₹500 or ₹1000 can grow substantially over time.
  2. Set the Investment Duration: Indicate how long you plan to keep investing (in years or months). Longer durations generally result in a higher corpus due to compounding.
  3. Enter Expected Rate of Return: This is the annual return rate you anticipate from the LIC mutual fund SIP. For example, 10%, 12%, or 15%. The exact rate is based on past fund performance and current market conditions.
  4. Calculate: Click the ‘calculate’ button to get instant results showing:
    • Total amount invested
    • Estimated returns earned
    • Maturity value (investment + returns)

Factors Affecting SIP Returns

While the SIP calculator gives a reliable estimate based on assumptions, actual returns depend on several variables:

  • Market Performance: Mutual fund returns fluctuate with market conditions.
  • Fund Manager Expertise: The performance of the LIC mutual fund scheme depends on the fund manager’s decisions.
  • Economic Factors: Inflation, interest rates, and economic growth impact returns.
  • Investment Duration: Longer investment tenures usually reduce short-term market volatility impacts.

It’s essential to remember that the calculator assumes a fixed rate of return, but actual returns may vary.
