What Is LIC Bonus Calculator?

The LIC Bonus Calculator is an online tool that helps you estimate the total bonus you are likely to get on your LIC policy at maturity. It calculates the approximate return you will receive based on:

  • The sum assured of your policy
  • The policy term (number of years)
  • The type of plan (endowment, money-back, or whole life policies)
  • The annual bonus rate declared by LIC

It gives you a clear picture of how much maturity value or extra profit your LIC policy will generate by the end of its term.

Example

Suppose you have an endowment policy with a sum assured of ₹5,00,000 and a term of 20 years. If LIC declares a reversionary bonus of ₹45 per ₹1,000 sum assured every year, your calculation will be:

Yearly Bonus = (Sum Assured / 1,000) × Bonus Rate
= (₹5,00,000 / 1,000) × ₹45
= ₹22,500 per year

So, over 20 years, the total accumulated bonus would be ₹22,500 × 20 = ₹4,50,000 (excluding any final additional bonus).

The LIC bonus plays a crucial role in increasing the final maturity or death benefit of a policy. Here’s why it matters:

How Does LIC Bonus Calculator Work?

The LIC Bonus Calculator works on a simple formula. You need three main inputs:

  1. Sum Assured (Base Policy Amount) – The guaranteed amount payable by LIC.
  2. Policy Term (Years) – Duration for which the policy runs.
  3. Bonus Rate (Annual Declared by LIC) – The bonus per ₹1,000 sum assured.

Types of LIC Bonuses

LIC offers different kinds of bonuses under its participating policies. Let’s go through each briefly:

1. Reversionary Bonus

This is the most common type, declared every year. Once declared, it gets attached to the policy and becomes payable at maturity or on death.

2. Interim Bonus

If a policy matures or the policyholder dies between two declaration dates, LIC pays an interim bonus as an approximate value for that short period.

3. Final Additional Bonus (FAB)

It is a one-time bonus added at the end of the policy term. It rewards long-term policyholders who stayed invested throughout the term.

4. Loyalty Additions

These are similar to bonuses but are usually given to long-standing customers or on specific plans as a form of reward.

How LIC Declares Bonuses

LIC calculates bonuses annually based on its surplus profits from investments and operations. A portion of the profits is shared with policyholders in the form of bonuses.

Steps involved:

  1. LIC evaluates total income and expenditures.
  2. The surplus amount is calculated.
  3. LIC’s Board decides the rate per ₹1,000 sum assured.
  4. The bonus is declared and credited to all participating policies.

Bonus Review Cycle

LIC usually declares bonuses after evaluating the previous financial year, i.e., between July and September every year.

How Bonus Affects Maturity Amount

The longer the policy term, the higher the total bonus. For instance:

  • A 10-year policy may earn ₹4–5 lakh bonus on ₹10 lakh sum assured.
  • A 25-year policy can accumulate double or more due to compounding benefits.

This shows how patience rewards long-term investors with higher maturity benefits.

LIC Participating vs. Non-Participating Plans

Not all LIC policies offer bonuses.

  • Participating Plans share profits and earn bonuses.
  • Non-Participating Plans do not earn bonuses but may offer guaranteed additions.

Example

LIC Jeevan Anand 915 is a participating plan.
LIC Tech Term is a non-participating plan (no bonus).

Thus, when choosing policies, check whether it includes bonus participation this makes a big difference in long-term returns.

How Bonus Is Paid

Bonuses are not paid annually in cash. Instead, they are accumulated and paid at:

  • Maturity (if the policyholder survives till term end).
  • Death Claim (if policyholder dies during the term).

So, bonuses essentially grow silently within your policy and boost your payout at the end.

Things to Remember About LIC Bonus

  • Bonus is not guaranteed; it depends on LIC’s annual performance.
  • Rates may vary year to year.
  • Early policy surrender might reduce or eliminate bonus benefits.
  • Always confirm with your LIC agent or branch for the latest declared rates.

LIC Bonus Withdraw Date or Crediting Time

Bonuses are not withdrawn separately by the policyholder.

  • They are credited internally to the policy record every year.
  • They are paid in full at the time of maturity or death claim.
    Thus, there is no specific “withdraw date” the amount is released at policy completion.

FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions

What is LIC Bonus Calculator?

It is an online tool to estimate the total bonus and maturity value on your LIC policy based on sum assured, term, and declared bonus rate.

How often does LIC declare bonuses?

LIC declares bonuses once a year, usually after March, during its financial review.

Can I withdraw the bonus before maturity?

No, bonuses are paid only at maturity or death claim. You cannot withdraw them separately during the term.

Is LIC bonus guaranteed every year?

Not necessarily. Bonuses depend on LIC’s annual profit performance, though LIC has a strong record of consistent declarations.

Do all LIC policies earn bonuses?

Only participating (with-profit) policies earn bonuses. Non-participating plans do not.

Are LIC bonuses taxable?

Bonuses received at maturity are tax-free under Section 10(10D) of the Income Tax Act, provided the premium conditions are met.

Where can I check current LIC bonus rates?

You can check on LIC’s official website (licindia.in) under the “Bonus Information” section or via trusted LIC agents.
