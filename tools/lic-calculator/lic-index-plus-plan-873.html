Investing in a life insurance plan that offers not just financial security but also wealth creation is a smart choice in today’s world. The LIC Index Plus Plan 873 is a Unit Linked Insurance Plan (ULIP) introduced by LIC of India that combines life insurance coverage with the opportunity to invest in top stock market indices like the Nifty 50 and Nifty 100.

It is designed for individuals who want to protect their family’s future while tapping into the potential growth of the equity markets.

What is LIC Index Plus Plan 873?

The LIC Index Plus Plan 873 is a modern life insurance plan that gives you the dual benefit of life cover and market-linked investments. Unlike traditional insurance plans that offer only a fixed return, this ULIP allows part of your premium to be invested in the stock market through two major indices—Nifty 50 and Nifty 100.

This plan was launched on February 6, 2024, and focuses on providing both insurance protection and wealth accumulation over the policy term through unit-linked funds.

Key Features of LIC Index Plus Plan 873

Understanding the key features helps in assessing if this plan suits your financial goals.

  • Life Insurance Cover: The plan provides a life cover to financially protect your family. In case of unfortunate demise, the nominee receives the higher of the sum assured or the fund value.
  • Unit Linked Plan: Part of your premium is invested in market-linked funds, meaning your returns depend on the performance of chosen indices (Nifty 50 or Nifty 100).
  • Two Fund Options:
    • Flexi Growth Fund linked to Nifty 100
    • Flexi Smart Growth Fund linked to Nifty 50
  • Flexible Premiums: You can choose to pay premiums monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or annually. The minimum annual premium is ₹30,000.
  • Sum Assured: The sum assured can be up to 10 times your annual premium based on age.
  • Guaranteed Additions: From the 6th policy year onwards, guaranteed additions are credited to your fund, increasing the investment value.
  • Partial Withdrawals: Allowed after completion of 5 years, with limits on the percentage of funds that can be withdrawn depending on policy year.
  • Switching Facility: You can switch funds up to 4 times a year without any charges, allowing you to rebalance your investments according to market conditions.
  • Refund of Mortality Charges: On survival till maturity, mortality charges deducted for life cover are refunded.
  • Optional Rider: LIC’s Linked Accidental Death Benefit Rider can be added for enhanced death benefits.
  • Surrender Option: Available after 5 years, allowing you to exit the policy and receive the fund value.

How Does LIC Index Plus Plan 873 Work?

When you invest in this plan, your premiums are split into two parts:

  • Cost of Insurance: A portion of your premium goes towards life insurance coverage, including mortality charges.
  • Investment in Units: The remaining amount buys units in the chosen fund (Nifty 50 or Nifty 100 linked fund).

The value of your units fluctuates according to stock market performance. At maturity, you get the fund value, including any guaranteed additions. In case of death, your nominee gets the higher of the sum assured or fund value, ensuring financial security.

Charges in LIC Index Plus Plan 873

Like all ULIPs, this plan has some costs deducted from premiums:

  • Premium Allocation Charge: Deducted from the premium to cover initial expenses.
  • Policy Administration Charge: Regularly deducted as a flat fee.
  • Fund Management Charge: Charged for managing your investments.
  • Mortality Charge: Covers life insurance risk cost; it is refunded at maturity if the policy survives.

Using the calculator, you can factor these charges into expected returns to get a realistic picture of benefits.​

Frequently Asked Questions

What is LIC Index Plus Plan 873?

LIC Index Plus Plan 873 is a Unit Linked Insurance Plan (ULIP) by LIC that combines life insurance coverage with investment in stock market indices like Nifty 50 and Nifty 100.

Who is eligible to buy the LIC Index Plus Plan 873?

Individuals aged between 90 days and 55 years can purchase this plan with policy terms ranging from 10 to 21 years depending on entry age.

What are the fund options available in this plan?

There are two fund options: Flexi Growth Fund linked to Nifty 100 and Flexi Smart Growth Fund linked to Nifty 50, both offering market-linked returns.

How much premium do I need to pay?

The minimum annual premium is ₹30,000. You can pay monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or annually as per your convenience.

Can I withdraw money from the plan before maturity?

Partial withdrawals are allowed only after 5 years of policy completion, subject to certain limits to maintain fund value.
