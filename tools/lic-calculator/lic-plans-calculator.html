How Does the LIC Plans Calculator Work?

The working of the LIC Plans Calculator is very simple. The tool follows a step-by-step method:

  1. Input basic details. You enter your age, gender, and city.
  2. Select a LIC plan. Choose the specific plan you are interested in (e.g.,LIC New Children’s Money Back Plan, LIC Jeevan Labh, etc.).
  3. Enter sum assured and policy term. This defines your expected coverage and duration.
  4. Select premium mode. Choose between monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or yearly payments.
  5. Click “Calculate.”
    The calculator instantly shows:
    • Premium payable
    • Maturity value
    • Bonus (if applicable)
    • Death benefit
    • Surrender value (if applicable)

You can try different combinations of sum assured or policy term to find your ideal plan and budget balance.

Factors That Affect Premium and Maturity Values

When you use LIC’s plan calculator, remember that results can vary depending on a few important factors:

  • Age: Younger people get lower premium rates.
  • Sum Assured: Higher coverage means higher premium.
  • Policy Term: Long-term plans usually give more bonuses.
  • Plan Type: Term plans cost less; endowment and money-back plans cost more.
  • Bonus Rate & Additions: Declared annually by LIC.
  • Payment Mode: Monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or annual each slightly changes the total cost.

Common Mistakes People Make While Calculating LIC Premium

Here are some common mistakes users make and how to avoid them:

  • Entering wrong age or term. Always double-check before calculation.
  • Ignoring policy type. Each policy has distinct rules for premiums and bonuses.
  • Forgetting GST. All LIC premiums include 4.5% GST for first-year premiums and 2.25% thereafter.
  • Assuming fixed bonus rates. LIC bonuses vary annually, so current numbers are only estimates.
  • Not entering correct mode (monthly/yearly). It affects total annual premium.

Avoiding these errors ensures more accurate and realistic expectations.
