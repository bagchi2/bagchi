Understanding the source and the number

E2NECC publishes market information by centre and distinguishes suggested prices from prevailing prices on its source page. The current Hospet record is labelled as Suggested in the local dataset. That source label is retained because it matters when comparing observations across dates. A reference price is not automatically a retail price, and a retail quote can include transport, handling, packaging, local demand, taxes or other commercial factors that are outside the published benchmark.

The historical series for Hospet currently contains 25 stored observations in the local dataset, beginning on 2026-08-01 and ending on 2026-08-25. They are not a forecast, and they should not be treated as a guarantee that the market must return to any particular level.

How dates and historical records are handled

Date discipline is especially important on a daily egg-rate website. The E2NECC sheet can include empty cells for dates that are not yet published, so the backfill process does not convert an empty cell into a false zero. Each historical point is tied to the calendar date represented by the source table. If an observation is absent, the history keeps the gap instead of fabricating a number merely to make the graph look continuous.
